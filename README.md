# Spendly: Personal Budgeting App (UX Case Study)

A mobile budgeting app prototype designed for Gen Z, built in Figma through user research, prototyping and usability testing.

**[▶ Try the interactive prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/YbLNWWIkYUk99rHXVX7Ygm?node-id=0-1)**

---

## The problem

Gen Z young adults and college students often manage their own money for the first time, whether it comes from family support or personal income. Many struggle to track their spending and end up overspending on shopping, travel, subscriptions and other lifestyle costs, sometimes leading to unexpected financial strain.

Standard bank apps show transactions, but they don't help users understand their spending behavior or control it.

## The solution

Spendly helps users cut unnecessary spending and stay in control of their finances without adding budgeting chores:

- **Monthly budgets** with category-level tracking
- **Spending forecasts** that warn you before you go over budget ("At your current rate, you're projected to exceed your budget by Nov 26")
- **Visual summaries** (charts and an overview of total budgets, average monthly spending and savings rate)
- **Low-balance notifications**

## Usability testing

**Method:** In-person moderated sessions with 3 participants, each completing 3 tasks:
1. Set a monthly budget
2. Identify the category with the most spending
3. Turn on a low-balance notification

**Measured:** task success, number of clicks, time on task, errors and confusion points, hesitation, verbal and behavioral reactions, and post-task ratings.

**Results:** All 3 participants completed every task. They described the app as simple, clean and easy to navigate, and liked that the charts gave a quick read on spending trends without being text-heavy.

**Issues found:**
- Confusion between the "Budget Forecast" and "Balance Forecast" options
- The navigation bar disappeared when scrolling
- Spending forecast warnings weren't noticeable enough
- The overview summary and notifications were hard to find

## What changed after testing

| Issue | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Forecast types were unclear | Added an info icon next to "Select Budget Type" explaining each option |
| Navigation hidden on scroll | Navigation bar now stays fixed on screen |
| Warnings easy to miss | Stronger red background on forecast warnings |
| Key info buried | Moved the overview summary to the home page and added a notifications button to every page |
